## Harbor Weather Notes

Mist covered the harbor before sunrise. A light wind carried drizzle across the eastern pier, and the quay lamps remained lit.

## Setting Observations

Wet cobblestones reflect a lamp more clearly than a face. From the eastern gate, the lamp lights the lock and crate but leaves the water in darkness. An engine can remain audible after the launch light disappears. This does not establish a route or boarding.

## Office notebook: two entries for cart seventeen

Mara copied a dispatch at eight in the evening. The ledger also lists cart seventeen as returned at six forty that evening, without a receiving signature. The earlier entry is squeezed into the margin. Reused boards, a copied number or a later ledger entry could explain the discrepancy; the notebook does not identify a culprit.

## Reference limits

These invented notes support a fictional scene; they are not historical evidence. The circa-1900 map and 1890–1900 harbour view are setting references. They cannot establish the lamp position, weather, warehouse layout or journey on this invented night in 1923.
