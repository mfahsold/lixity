A three-minute first look

Read one manuscript, inspect a passage, follow its research and choose a PDF copy. The same manuscript and records appear throughout this short walkthrough.

Maintained by the Lixity project · Updated 21 October 2026

First lookInstallationInterpret resultsCompare toolsSources and PDF/OCR

1. Open the example and read a chapter

Open the demo project

The Crate at Warehouse 4 has six invented English chapters, one research archive and four prepared PDF layouts. Read chapter 1's setting and chapter 2's action. No installation, account or upload is needed. The read-only demo cannot import, edit or save; actions marked Local app require installation.

2. Inspect one difference

Choose a paragraph's … → Values to open its Passage values inspector. The separate Analysis switch shows diagnostics across the manuscript; Passage values remains usable with Analysis On or Off. Use Analysis overview to compare sentence rhythm in the setting and action chapters, then Back to passage to resume reading.

On current main (Unreleased): expand Manuscript measurements for the secondary numbers, then collapse the same summary to return to the compact overview. A linked scene title reveals its detailed Scene measurements; both disclosure levels can be closed again. The passage return keeps your earlier reading context.

Ask what changed: the kind of scene, what the character is doing, or how the sentences are arranged. These short chapters demonstrate a workflow; their numbers are not a quality verdict. Interpretation explains the signals. Reader controls and coverage covers text size, focus, keyboard access and supported formatting.

3. Follow a quotation into research

Open Research → Dossiers and View details on Warehouse 4 case notes. Inspect its historical images or choose Dossier alongside to keep it beside the manuscript.

In Review, follow the saved quotation, “Someone had tried the eastern gate of Warehouse 4.” Go to manuscript passage returns to exactly matched text; Open claim shows its pinned claim and cited sources. When you follow a connection from a manuscript paragraph, close the record viewer and use Back to passage to return there.

The retained passage, authored claim and manuscript quote are separate records. People, events and journeys are fictional; historical images do not verify them. Maps use OpenStreetMap and send the viewed area and client IP to its tile provider. Turn off the background or choose the sketch for offline use. Research workflows explains explicit retention and saving in the local app.

4. Choose a PDF copy

Compare the same manuscript as an editorial copy, reading copy, submission sheets or analysis report. The PDF guide explains their purposes and limits.

Use your own project

Install the local app, then open your project or preview a manuscript import. Analysis runs on your computer. JavaScript enables the demo's interactive controls; manuscript text, tables, credits and PDF links remain readable without it.
