# A three-minute first look

Open one marked sentence. See which words counted, read the sentence in its scene, and decide whether to keep it as it is or revisit it.

[Kurze Einführung auf Deutsch](first-look-de.html)

Updated 25 November 2026

This walkthrough follows the generated demo. It uses the same reading website export available in **Project actions → Reading website**. Its manuscript, analysis and chosen Research are a fixed snapshot; importing and saving require a local project. See [how to make your own reading website](research-pdf.html#reading-website).

## 1. Check a marked sentence

[Open the demo project](../demo/report.html)

1. Open **Try the draft examples** and choose **Check a sticky sentence** to jump to the marked sentence.

2. For that paragraph, choose **… → Paragraph details → Explain this passage**. Under **Why this is marked**, see the 22 function words that Lixity counted among 31 words. Read the surrounding paragraph and check whether the connections are easy to follow.

3. Press Escape or close the explanation to continue reading. In your draft, keep the wording if it fits the scene, or revise it and compare the versions using the same settings.

Use the [browser exercise](../sandbox/index.html) to edit a sample paragraph and see its simplified counts change.

<a id="contrast"></a>

## 2. Compare the rhythm of two scenes

*The Crate at Warehouse 4* has six invented English chapters. In **Try the draft examples**, choose **Inspect chapter 2**, then read the action scene. **Compare chapters 1 and 2** opens the sentence-length overview.

> A board moved. Mara turned. The crate tipped towards the drain.

1. Read the first paragraph of **The Loose Board**. What is Mara doing, and where do you pause?

2. Choose its **… → Paragraph details → Explain this passage**. The card shows the selected sentence. “A board moved.” counts as three words. Read **Why this is marked** and **What to check while reading**.

3. Read chapter 1's harbour description using the **opening chapter** link in the task card. Compare the reading experience, not just the averages.

**A possible decision:** keep the short beats because they separate physical actions. Or, if the scene sounds mechanically chopped when read aloud, try joining an action to its consequence in your own draft. The measurement explains the difference; it cannot decide whether the rhythm works. These short synthetic chapters are not a calibration corpus.

You have completed the first check when you can explain the contrast in the text. Open **Try the draft examples** in the demo to find a wordy sentence, a repeated phrase across three speakers and a shift from past to present. [Use the same workflow with your manuscript](installation.html).

**More controls, marks and chapter comparisons**

Turn on **Analysis** and choose what to inspect. **Text matches** opens counted words or sentences. Under **Reading marks**, choose **Underline counted passages** for marks in the prose or **Mark differing paragraphs** for comparisons within a chapter. Escape closes the evidence card. **How it’s measured** opens the full method.

For a full-manuscript view, open **Analysis overview**. In the development demo, it starts at **Manuscript measurements**. Choose **Review passages**, **Compare chapters** or **Explore patterns** to go deeper. **Back to passage** restores your reading position.

In **Sentence rhythm**, choose a length group to read a counted sentence. In **Motifs & repetition**, open a recurring phrase to move between its occurrences. The reading toolbar takes you through the examples. [Read the mark key](interpretation.html#analysis-marks) or see the [first-session steps](https://github.com/mfahsold/lixity/blob/main/docs/ONBOARDING.md#read-and-explore).

<a id="decision"></a>

## 3. Follow a quotation into research

> Someone had tried the eastern gate of Warehouse 4.

**In the read-only demo:** this opening quotation has an explicitly saved association. Follow it from the manuscript:

1. At the opening paragraph, choose **… → Paragraph details → Research for this passage**. The right context shows its linked claim and dossier while the text stays visible.

2. Open a title to read the current entry and its sources, or choose **Linked version** for the exact earlier version used by the association. **Versions** expands its history without changing the entry. A retained source and an authored claim are separate records; the association does not establish factual truth.

3. Close the viewer and use **Back to passage** to resume reading. On narrow screens the context panel sits above the text; its close button or Escape returns you to the paragraph.

**Explore dossiers and review**

In [**Research → Dossiers**](../demo/report.html#research-manager), open **Warehouse 4 case notes**. Its **Sections** list follows the actual headings; close and reopen it as needed. Choose **Dossier alongside** to keep the dossier beside the manuscript.

From an open dossier, **Back to dossier** restores its saved version and reading position after you follow a connection. **Review** also lists the saved quotation; **Go to manuscript passage** follows its exact text match. [Reader guidance](https://github.com/mfahsold/lixity/blob/main/docs/WORKSPACE.md#reading-paragraphs) covers controls and keyboard shortcuts.

In **Research → Claims**, open the timeline to compare the dated records. The watchman’s report, the dispatch and the corrected inspection belong to different moments. The visit with an unknown date stays separate.

People, events and journeys are fictional. The historical images do not verify them. Maps in this website use local sketches and geographic shapes without requesting online map tiles. [Sources and PDF copies](research-pdf.html) explains retention and saving in the local app.

## Choose the reader’s route

- [Review workspace:](../demo/report.html) read the manuscript with its analysis and included research records.

- [Novel with companion notes:](../demo/reader/index.html) read continuous prose, confirm chapter progress and open the selected background. The Analysis link opens the measurements deliberately included in this copy.

- [Standalone companion:](../demo/companion/index.html) explore those selected notes while reading the novel elsewhere.

A reading copy is fixed. Reading progress hides or reveals included notes, though the underlying files remain in the export. In your own export, use a chapter cap to omit later material. **Since 0.8.1:** `--include-audio` adds already recorded chapter WAVs matching the current manuscript, with a shared player and download. It does not start a speech worker or include private voice references. [Audio inclusion and limits](https://github.com/mfahsold/lixity/blob/main/docs/WORKSPACE.md#include-existing-chapter-recordings-unreleased).

Open **Chapter audio** to hear the first two chapters in a synthetic English preset voice, or download their WAV files. In the author workspace, open **Research → Dossiers → Warehouse 4 exhibit book** to play the watchman’s captioned statement and read its transcript. The reader and companion reveal the statement after chapter three. The dispatch receipt and ledger appear later in the full reading copy.

## Use your own project

Manuscript input supports Markdown (`.md`, `.markdown`) and plain text (`.txt`). The file importer also accepts one Word/DOCX file. Export Scrivener documents to a supported file format first; inspect the import preview before creating a project. PDFs can be retained separately as Research sources.

[Install the local app](installation.html) and launch the workspace. Choose **Open Project** to continue with an existing folder. Choose **Analyze a manuscript** to review a copy before creating a new project. Confirm the analysis language. See [running the local service](installation.html#restart) if the browser reopens an older workspace.

<a id="audio"></a>

## Hear a passage in your own project

Open **Project → Settings → Audio and services**, or use **Listen & direct** in the manuscript toolbar. The public demo cannot request your microphone, save a reference or generate speech.

Start with **Teleprompter** to rehearse a passage. **Author voice** is where you can review a recorded or imported reference take. To set project defaults or connect a voice service, use the [audio setup guide](https://github.com/mfahsold/lixity/blob/main/docs/AUDIO.md). The controls depend on the installed build and the microphone or service available to it.

The state beside the recording button shows pending permission, active recording or a failed start. Remove a saved microphone block using the icon beside the address, then retry and allow access. Failed starts log diagnostics in the browser console. A browser permission refusal sends no server request, so it produces no service-log entry. See [recording and troubleshooting](https://github.com/mfahsold/lixity/blob/main/docs/AUDIO.md#author-voice-recording-and-listening).

In **Listen & direct → Export direction**, prepare JSON and SSML for a passage, chapter or manuscript without contacting a speech service. JSON retains direction details; SSML reports controls it cannot carry portably. **Generate audio** uses a configured speech service. Locally inspecting an external recording can compare text only when a timestamped transcript for that recording is supplied. See the [audio guide](https://github.com/mfahsold/lixity/blob/main/docs/AUDIO.md#audio-export-and-external-providers-unreleased) for setup and limits.

Read a passage in the **Teleprompter** (`P`), record a take and edit the words actually spoken. WAV plus JSON export preserves the audio hash and whether the transcript is missing, a draft or confirmed. Sending the take to **Author voice** opens a review; it does not save automatically.

**Optional IMS-Toucan, available in 0.8.1:** German and English author-reference voices accept separate IPA overrides and structured passage-level duration and prosody controls. Free acting prompts are unsupported by this engine. Other workers have different capabilities; preview a short passage before a long render. [Setup and model limits](https://github.com/mfahsold/lixity/blob/main/docs/INSTALLATION.md#ims-toucan-author-voice-and-phonetic-direction).

In the local app, open **Project** for a read-only overview of the selected manuscript, research folder and existing outputs. It inspects metadata without rebuilding analysis; where an output came from and how it was generated remain unknown. The exported demo has no local project-folder view. [Project inspection and CLI](https://github.com/mfahsold/lixity/blob/main/docs/WORKSPACE.md#inspect-project-structure).
