Lixity is a local manuscript analysis tool for authors, editors and researchers. Compare sentence rhythm, vocabulary, dialogue and tense across chapters, then open paragraphs to read the text, source lines and local measurements. Does a change serve the scene?

Built-in analysis runs on your computer without cloud calls or an account. It does not rewrite your prose.

What makes it different

Four choices shape every number and every screen.

🔒

On your computer

Built-in analysis and report generation work offline. Optional integrations and separately configured workers have their own setup and data handling.

📐

Your book is the yardstick

Chapters are compared against your manuscript's own middle values and spread. A deliberate change of voice or register can explain a difference.

🔍

Read passages in context

Chapter measurements describe whole chapters. Paragraph panels show the text, source lines and separate local measurements, with a place for your notes.

⚖️

Honest limits

Short chapters are marked "not comparable" instead of ranked. Signals support your review; they are not a quality score.

Understand your writing

Sentence rhythm, vocabulary, readability, dialogue dynamics and tense — measured per chapter, with the data requirements stated where they matter.

Density values count word-pattern matches per 1,000 words; they are heuristic counts, not a grammatical analysis. Where a value cannot say something useful, the panel explains why instead of showing a number.

How to read the results

Compare chapters in context

The chapter map shows where the book is noticeably different from itself. Colours give the direction; the tooltip names the measure and the value.

Comparisons use the book's own middle values and typical spread, with an adjustment for estimated sampling noise. Chapter comparisons need at least the documented sample size; the panel says so instead of guessing.

Keep review notes beside the text

The review loop starts at the flagged list: every paragraph that meets your severity threshold, sorted by strength and line. Click a row and the passage opens, with its source lines and measurements.

Notes you write stay in the manuscript as Markdown comments — invisible while reading, listed later in the work markers. Nothing is rewritten for you.

Connect sources, claims and decisions

Use Zotero for collecting sources, reading and general notes. Lixity's experimental research workspace connects selected evidence to claims, authorial decisions and manuscript quotations. Dossiers gather material for a topic or scene.

Capture selected Zotero attachments or import authorized local files. Retained versions preserve the evidence used by a claim independently of Zotero; a saved quotation does not establish that it is true.

The timeline is a view inside Claims & evidence. Edits outside a quote can be re-pinned explicitly; an edited quote stays in review, because only you can decide whether the meaning survived. Follow the research workflow.

Install the current release

These terminal commands use uv to install the published package from PyPI with managed Python. No Git or separate Python install is needed; the installation guide covers Windows, macOS and Linux, the pinned release wheel with checksums, and updates or removal.

uv tool install --python 3.12 --managed-python lixity
uv tool update-shell

Open a new terminal, then verify and start the app:

lixity --version
lixity service start --open

The app starts an empty local workspace: choose Open Project or Analyze a manuscript, confirm the language and read a passage. English is the default; automatic detection is an explicit choice. Use lixity service status, lixity service restart or lixity service stop for the managed instance.

A scientific foundation, with stated limits

Lixity measures language patterns; it does not grade literary quality or prescribe a style. A deliberate change in voice, register or subject can explain a signal.

Chapter comparisons use the book's middle values and typical spread (median and MAD), with an adjustment for estimated sampling noise. Colours show the direction of a difference. Optional statistical checks mark a selection for review; you decide what the passages mean for the book.

Documentation is English; reports and the interface offer seven languages. German and English have the most extensive linguistic resources and language-specific fixtures. French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese and Dutch have narrower heuristic coverage and validation. Mathematical identifiers stay language-neutral.

Common questions

Does my manuscript leave my computer?

Built-in analysis and dashboard generation run on your computer without cloud calls. Optional integrations and separately configured workers have their own setup and data handling.

Will Lixity change my prose?

Analysis describes patterns and links to passages. It does not rewrite prose. Review notes and research decisions are actions you choose yourself.

Can a high score prove that a book is good?

No. A number describes one measured aspect of this text under particular assumptions. Read the affected passages and consider their purpose. Short texts, dialect, historical spelling and mixed languages need particular care.
