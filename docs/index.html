From passage to context

Read and review

Continuous chapters, passage measurements and work markers. Turn analysis on or off; keep your manuscript in view.

Compare chapters

Explore rhythm, vocabulary, dialogue and tense against your book’s own patterns. Measurements support review; they do not grade literary quality.

Keep research alongside

Connect retained sources, claims and exact manuscript quotations in an experimental research workspace. Open a dossier beside the passage.

Built-in analysis runs on your computer. Optional integrations have their own data boundaries. Analysis does not rewrite your prose.

Website measurement

This public website uses GoatCounter to count page visits and named demo, installation, repository and PDF links. The adapter sends public page paths and event names, excluding query strings, fragments, form contents and manuscript selections. The local app and exported manuscript reports contain no such measurement.

Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control disable collection. Counts are approximate and do not establish installations or active authors. GoatCounter receives technical request data; see its privacy information. For questions, use the contact below.
