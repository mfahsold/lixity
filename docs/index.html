Architecture & Foundations

The 5 Core Pillars of Lixity

Lixity unifies mathematically rigorous text linguistics, narratological dramaturgy, typographic publication engineering, and source-critical evidence into a single stateless engine.

Pillar 01
📊

Stylistics & Corpus Diagnostics

Precise evaluation of macro- and micro-structural sentence architecture: Average Sentence Length (ASL), rhythm Coefficient of Variation (CV), 4 syntactic length tiers (staccato to hypotaxis), 7 language-calibrated readability indices (Flesch, Amstad, LIX), and length-invariant lexical diversity (HD-D, MTLD, Yule's K, Maas a²).

ASL / CV HD-D / MTLD Yule's K Flesch-Amstad
Pillar 02
⏱️

Dramaturgy & Tense Profiling

Paragraph-accurate tense continuity without heuristic guessing: detects unintended slips between retrospective epic past and immediate scenic present. Rates tense mixtures across a 4-tier friction severity scale (0–3). Evaluates dialogue ratios, turn lengths, pacing curves, and chapter tension hooks.

Present / Past Friction 0–3 Dialogue Turns Pacing Hooks
Pillar 03
🎯

Robust 3D Style Space

No external dogma: the manuscript is its own baseline. House style is derived robustly via median ± 2 MAD. Chapter deviations are noise-adjusted via analytical z* shrinkage and controlled via Benjamini–Hochberg / BY FDR. Unsupervised latent style axes and interactive 3D narrative trajectories via cyclic Jacobi EVD.

Median ± 2 MAD z* Shrinkage BH/BY FDR 3D Jacobi-EVD
Pillar 04
📖

Typographic Publication

Publication-ready multi-format exports directly from Markdown: Cairo & Pango vector PDF engine with subpixel metrics, font hinting decoupling, and collision-free tracking. Produces standard paperback proofs (135×205 mm KiWi/Suhrkamp formats), editor A4 proofs with line numbers, 9:16 mobile PDFs, and valid EPUB 3.3 with dual NCX/nav navigation.

Practical Macro-Editing

Macro-Editing Matrix & Editorial Heuristics

Statistical metrics are not an objective quality score or an instruction to rewrite prose; they serve as diagnostic prompts for human macro-editing. Lixity maps statistical signals directly to concrete editorial questions and editing interventions.

Linguistic Feature Calculation & Signal Editorial Role & Application Warning Signal & Intervention
Sentence Rhythm & Length Tiers ASL, rhythm CV, staccato (≤6w), hypotaxis (>25w) Pacing & Prose Breath: Verifies whether dialogue or action sequences bounce dynamically or suffocate under monotonous clause stacks. High CV (> 0.70) indicates organic prose. Low CV (< 0.40) flags rhythmic monotony (e.g., chains of subject-verb-object main clauses). Sudden hypotaxis spikes stall fast-paced action scenes.
Length-Invariant Lexical Richness Hypergeometric HD-D (McCarthy & Jarvis), MTLD, Yule's K Vocabulary Freshness without Sample-Size Bias: Unlike raw TTR, HD-D does not penalize or inflate short chapters. Assesses whether vocabulary remains rich across 50,000+ words. Pronounced drop in HD-D in late chapters exposes author exhaustion. Artificially high HD-D (> 0.90) warns of strained, unnatural thesaurus hunting.
Tense Continuity & Friction Present, past, friction rating 0–3 Timeline & Perspective Discipline: Detects subconscious drift between retrospective epic past and immediate scenic present at paragraph resolution. Friction score 2–3 (e.g., past-tense narrative abruptly mixing with unquoted present-tense clauses) requires verification for accidental tense errors.
Showing vs. Telling & Filters Perception verbs, passive voice, nominal style Sensory Immersion: Identifies perception filters (saw, heard, felt, noticed) that place an unnecessary buffer between reader and protagonist instead of showing the scene directly. High filter density turns visceral drama into detached police reports. High passive and nominal suffix ratios stifle narrative propulsion.
FDR Significance Heatmap Noise-aware z* shrinkage, Benjamini-Hochberg (●), Cliff's δ False-Discovery Protection: Prevents statistical over-interpretation. A black dot (●) marks strictly confirmed macro departures under false discovery rate control. Only confirmed cells (●) with substantial effect size (Cliff's |δ| > 0.47) warrant deep rewrites; mild fluctuations reflect natural artistic variation.
Structural & Drift Diagnostics PELT changepoints (BIC), Mann-Kendall τ, Wasserstein-KS Narrative Architecture & Regime Shifts: PELT locates unannounced stylistic regime shifts (e.g., tone rupture at Act II); Mann-Kendall identifies slow, cumulative drift across the manuscript. Unexpected PELT changepoints uncover author handovers, long writing hiatuses, or tonal inconsistency across character arcs.
Persistent Work Markers <!-- LIXITY-MARKER id="..." kind="pruefen" --> Traceable Editorial Annotations: Notes remain anchored right at the target paragraph in Markdown, but vanish cleanly in print (PDF) and digital (EPUB) book exports. Content hashes ensure editorial anchors remain intact even as surrounding text shifts during iterative manuscript revision.

Evidence-Based Research & Verifiable Citation Chain

Historical novelists, investigative journalists, and scholarly writers face a monumental challenge: thousands of primary sources, archival records, and witness accounts must remain verifiable across years of drafting. A generic notebook or opaque vector store is insufficient – Lixity strictly separates archival evidence, cultural context, and literary invention.

1. Immutable Text Archive

Source texts are stored as verbatim UTF-8 bytes with SHA-256 digests. --allow-retention explicitly confirms local storage rights. No silent normalization, zero data loss.

2. Versioned Source Criticism

Captures composition era, depicted period, location, genre, narrative perspective, and transmission state. Primary archival sources and modern secondary interpretations remain distinct.

3. Verifiable Passage Citations

Every historical claim links to an immutable paragraph (with deterministic passage UUID) and exact character offsets. Citations remain stable even as manuscript text shifts.

4. Governed Lifecycle

Cleanly distinguishes between Withdrawal (suppressing contested sources from standard search while preserving citation history) and Purge (permanent cryptographic removal with dry-run verification).

Terminal Research Workflow (CLI)

# 1. Initialize a dedicated research workspace
lixity research init --project ./novel-research --title "1920s Archive" --language en

# 2. Ingest primary source with cultural source criticism and explicit retention
lixity research ingest --project ./novel-research --file ./sources/police_log_1923.txt \
  --context '{"genre": "Police Log", "epoch": "1923", "location": "Hamburg", "provenance": "State Archives"}' \
  --allow-retention

# 3. Build SQLite FTS5 lexical index
lixity research reindex --project ./novel-research

# 4. Fast lexical full-text search with BM25 ranking
lixity research search --project ./novel-research --query "dockyard customs" --limit 5

# 5. Resolve immutable passage citation (paragraph, exact offset, source metadata)
lixity research cite --project ./novel-research --passage urn:uuid:PASSAGE-UUID-HERE

# 6. Run linguistic source analysis and generate standalone source dashboard
lixity research analyze --project ./novel-research --source-id urn:uuid:SOURCE-UUID-HERE
lixity research dashboard --project ./novel-research --source-id urn:uuid:SOURCE-UUID-HERE > source_report.html

# 7. Cross-corpus grounding against manuscript & create dossiers
lixity research compare --project ./novel-research --source-id urn:uuid:SOURCE-UUID-HERE --manuscript ./manuscript.md
lixity research dossier --project ./novel-research --title "Harbor Evidence" --body "Summary..." --tags "harbor"

# 8. Interactive research dashboard with real-time FTS5 search & visual dossier editor
lixity serve --research-project ./novel-research --no-project

# 9. Audit repository integrity and snapshot checksums
lixity research audit --project ./novel-research

Developers & Autonomous Agents

CLI Commands & Python API for AI Agents

Lixity offers deterministic, versioned JSON contracts (analyze/profile v2, style v4) and stable POSIX exit codes for autonomous coding and editing agents.

Command-Line Interface (CLI)

# Analyze manuscript (rich terminal report or JSON)
lixity analyze manuscript.md              # Visual terminal report
lixity analyze manuscript.md --json       # Machine-readable JSON (schema v2)

# Tense profiling & narrative dynamics
lixity profile manuscript.md              # Paragraph-accurate tense & style profile
lixity dialogue manuscript.md             # Speech ratios & dialogue turns
lixity characters manuscript.md --names "Anna,Ralf" # Character presence
lixity pacing manuscript.md               # Scene tempo & chapter hooks
lixity showing manuscript.md              # Showing vs. telling balance

# Self-calibrating style reference & structural diagnostics
lixity style manuscript.md                # Median/MAD bounds, z*, FDR matrix
lixity style manuscript.md --json --fdr-method by --min-chapters 4

# Interactive dashboard & idempotent workspace build
lixity dashboard manuscript.md -o exports/dashboard.html # Standalone single-file HTML
lixity build manuscript.md --language en  # Idempotent workspace build with archive rotation

Python API for AI Agents

from lixity import api

# 1. Compute corpus KPIs & readability
metrics = api.analyze(text, language="auto")

# 2. Self-calibrating style reference with FDR & structural diagnostics (schema v4)
reference = api.fingerprint(text, language="en")

# 3. Add persistent editorial work markers
updated_text, marker = api.add_marker(text, kind="pruefen", note="Verify tense shift", line=42)

# 4. Generate standalone interactive dashboard
dashboard_html = api.dashboard(text, language="en", title="My Novel")

Setup & Get Started

Installation & Quickstart

Install Lixity in an isolated CLI environment on Linux, macOS, or Windows using uv (or pipx). No account, no API key, and no cloud manuscript uploads required. 

# Install via uv (recommended; requires Git & uv)
uv tool install --python 3.12 "git+https://github.com/mfahsold/lixity.git@v1.15.0"

# Verify installation
lixity --version
lixity about

# Build your first manuscript dashboard
lixity build manuscript.md --language en

Open exports/manuscript_dashboard.html in any modern browser. Full setup guide for Windows/macOS, shell completions, and troubleshooting: docs/INSTALLATION.md.
