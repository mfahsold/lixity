Notice a change in rhythm

The opening chapter averages 13.6 words per sentence; the next, 6.0. Does the shorter rhythm suit its action? Compare the chapters and return to their text.

Follow the saved research

That quotation is explicitly linked to an authored claim and retained source. At the passage, open … → Saved research, read the saved version and return to the manuscript. Follow the connection.

Measurements guide your reading; they do not grade or rewrite prose. A saved source preserves the evidence used, not a guarantee that the claim is true.

Try it with your own manuscript

Open a Markdown manuscript in the local app, keep your review notes and make a reading or editorial PDF. Built-in analysis works offline; optional integrations have separate data boundaries.

Website measurement

This public website uses GoatCounter to count page visits and named demo, installation, repository and PDF links. The adapter sends public page paths and event names, excluding query strings, fragments, form contents and manuscript selections. The local app and exported manuscript reports contain no such measurement.

Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control disable collection. Counts are approximate and do not establish installations or active authors. GoatCounter receives technical request data; see its privacy information. For questions, use the contact below.
