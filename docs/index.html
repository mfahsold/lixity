Lixity is a local workbench for authors. Explore language patterns and keep sources, claims and notes beside your manuscript.

Read, investigate, review

Read and explore your manuscript

Compare sentence rhythm, repeated words and dialogue across chapters. Turn analysis on to inspect counted words and sentences in context. Read the colors and marks.

Someone had tried the eastern gate of Warehouse 4.

Read the opening passage in the invented example. Does the shorter rhythm in the next chapter suit its action? Compare the chapters and read the passages behind the numbers.

Keep the research behind a passage

Keep quotations, source images and maps in dossiers. Link a claim to an exact manuscript passage, inspect its evidence and author decisions beside the text, then return to reading. Follow the example connection.

Make a copy for reading or review

Share a novel with selected background notes, a standalone companion, or a PDF for editorial notes. Companion material can appear after each completed chapter. Your original project stays unchanged. Export a reading website · Compare PDF copies.

Measurements guide your reading; they do not grade or rewrite prose. A saved source preserves the evidence used, not a guarantee that the claim is true.

Try it with your own manuscript

Open your Markdown manuscript in the local app. Built-in analysis works offline on your computer; optional integrations have separate data boundaries.

Install Lixity

Work from the command line

Use the same local engine in a terminal or script. These commands work without a running service.

Inspect a project's files

The local app's Project overview identifies the manuscript, research folder and existing outputs. The CLI provides the same read-only inspection.

lixity project --project ./novel

Inspection reads metadata; existing outputs have unknown source and generation history. Scope and limits.

Analyze a manuscript

Print a terminal report with sentence rhythm, vocabulary, readability and dialogue measurements. Choose the manuscript's analysis language explicitly.

lixity analyze manuscript.md --language en

Website measurement

This public website uses GoatCounter to count page visits and named demo, installation, repository and PDF links. The adapter sends public page paths and event names, excluding query strings, fragments, form contents and manuscript selections. The local app and exported manuscript reports contain no such measurement.

Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control disable collection. Counts are approximate and do not establish installations or active authors. GoatCounter receives technical request data; see its privacy information. For questions, use the contact below.
