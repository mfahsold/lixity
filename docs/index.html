What changes from chapter to chapter?

Lixity helps you explore rhythm, repeated words and dialogue in your manuscript, then return to the passages behind the numbers.

Three questions for your draft

Where does the rhythm change?

Compare sentence lengths and dialogue across chapters, then read the passages in context.

Which words keep coming back?

Review frequent words and repeated phrases in the manuscript, and decide which repetitions are intentional.

Where did I put that source?

Keep saved notes and excerpts beside the text in a research dossier. Add passage links explicitly; retained evidence does not establish factual truth.

Try it with your own manuscript

Built-in analysis works offline on your computer. Lixity does not grade or rewrite your prose. Optional integrations have their own data boundaries.

Website measurement

This public website uses GoatCounter to count page visits and named demo, installation, repository and PDF links. The adapter sends public page paths and event names, excluding query strings, fragments, form contents and manuscript selections. The local app and exported manuscript reports contain no such measurement.

Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control disable collection. Counts are approximate and do not establish installations or active authors. GoatCounter receives technical request data; see its privacy information. For questions, use the contact below.
