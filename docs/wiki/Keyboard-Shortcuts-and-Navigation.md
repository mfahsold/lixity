# Keyboard shortcuts and navigation

Open **Reading settings → Keyboard shortcuts** to see the options available in
your installation. Shortcuts depend on where focus is and which dialog, editor or
reading tool is open.

- [Keyboard, touch and focus reading](https://github.com/mfahsold/lixity/blob/main/docs/WORKSPACE.md#keyboard-touch-and-focus-reading)
  explains keyboard access to the manuscript, passage tools and contextual work.
- [Read and explore](https://github.com/mfahsold/lixity/blob/main/docs/ONBOARDING.md#read-and-explore)
  walks through a first passage and its analysis.
- [Teleprompter](https://github.com/mfahsold/lixity/blob/main/docs/AUDIO.md#manuscript-teleprompter-direction-cues-phonetics-and-dossier-context)
  covers the keys for reading aloud and recording.
