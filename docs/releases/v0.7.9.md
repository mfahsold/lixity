# Lixity v0.7.9

Released 5 November 2026. This release introduces open knowledge interoperability,
Obsidian-inspired reader ergonomics, unified Lucide icon primitives, tokenized LaTeX
formula rendering, voice studio workflows and offline speech tools across Lixity.

## Obsidian & Minimal Theme Ergonomics

- **Command Palette (`Ctrl/Cmd+P`, `Ctrl/Cmd+K`):** Fast keyboard-first access to
  manuscript analysis metrics, research queries, dossier authoring, Audio Studio,
  teleprompter controls, visual canvas exports, and theme toggling.
- **Context Backlinks:** Inspecting any manuscript passage automatically surfaces
  associated research dossiers, anchored historical evidence, claims, and vocal direction
  cues in the reader inspector.
- **Focus Mode (`F`):** Distraction-free reading with serene typography, centered prose,
  hidden sidebars, and optional inline metric reveals.
- **Form & Status Design:** Quiet typography, standardized field spacing, and live pulsing
  status dots for background synthesis and service states following Obsidian Minimal Theme aesthetics.
- **Embedded Dossier Editor:** In-browser Markdown editing component (`LixityMarkdownEditor`)
  with formatting toolbar, shortcuts (`Ctrl+B/I/K/H`, `Tab`), smart list continuation,
  auto-closing bracket pairs, and live character/word counters.
- **Curated Lucide SVG Icons:** Unified inline icon library (`window.LixityIcons`) providing
  crisp, standardized visuals across callouts, navigation, audio controls, and editor actions.

## Markdown & Mathematical Notation

- **Tokenized LaTeX Formula Rendering:** Robust rendering for inline and display LaTeX math,
  supporting environments (`matrix`, `cases`, `aligned`), balanced fractions (`\frac`, `\dfrac`),
  balanced roots (`\sqrt[n]`), font styles (`\mathbb`, `\mathbf`, `\mathcal`), Greek characters,
  and mathematical operators without HTML injection vulnerabilities.
- **Extended Markdown Syntax:** Full support for Obsidian-style collapsible callouts
  (`> [!NOTE]-`), wikilinks (`[[Target|Label]]`), text highlights (`==text==`), and
  strikethrough (`~~text~~`).

## Interoperability & Research Ingestion

- **JSON Canvas 1.0:** Full support for the open [JSON Canvas 1.0](https://jsoncanvas.org/spec/1.0/)
  format. Export narrative storyboard scenes (`lixity canvas`) and research knowledge graphs
  (`lixity research canvas`) directly into Obsidian Canvas or any canvas-compatible tool.
- **Offline Web Article Extraction:** Ingest research materials from saved web HTML files
  into structured Markdown dossiers with metadata and research frontmatter (`lixity research extract-article`),
  inspired by the Defuddle engine.

## Author Voice Studio & Direction

- **Chapter Audio Exports & Imports:** Verified existing chapter recordings can be exported
  alongside reading websites (`--include-audio`) with the shared player and WAV downloads.
- **Take Management & Studio:** Author takes with explicit WAV/transcript metadata exchange,
  synchronized teleprompter pacing, vocal cues, and IPA overrides.
- **IMS-Toucan Worker:** Optional offline worker for author voice references, German/English IPA
  pronunciations, and structured prosody.
